AI Champions Hub — Demo Narration

AI Champions Hub. A Power Apps code app that runs your company's AI adoption program. Here's a quick tour.

First, the program manager experience.

The home dashboard opens with a live pulse of the program — active champions, running campaigns, completed activities, and campaigns at risk.

The Champions directory is your whole roster at a glance — department, points and status — and you can add, edit, disable, or remove any champion in seconds.

Campaigns are grouped by status — active, draft, expired and completed — each with a health indicator so nothing slips.

Open any campaign to manage its activities, participants and timeline in one place.

Activities live inside campaigns. Managers create them and review the point claims champions submit.

Events bring the community together — workshops, office hours and demos, all scheduled from one place.

A company-wide leaderboard adds a little friendly competition — a great way to keep the momentum high.

Champions' requests for licenses, connectors and A I support land here for the team to triage.

The Reports workspace rolls it all up — adoption, points by department, approval rates and campaign health — exportable to C S V or P D F.

Make it yours — the Customize workspace themes the whole app with your own colors, fonts and branding, no code required.

In Settings you configure the program and promote or demote admins, with a protected default admin that can never be removed.

You can even tailor the request categories champions choose from — add, rename or remove them at any time.

Now, the champion experience.

Champions get a personalized home — their points, their rank, pending claims and the campaigns they've joined.

They start in the campaign gallery — bright banner art, departments and progress, all at a glance.

They browse active campaigns and join the ones that interest them.

Activities unlock once they join a campaign, keeping everyone focused on live work.

Each claim is tracked from submitted to approved — and every activity can be claimed only once.

Need something? They raise a request, choosing from the categories the admin configured.

And they can follow every request they've raised, right through to resolution.

And a friendly leaderboard celebrates the champions leading A I adoption across the company.

Champions can personalize their own view too — light or dark, font and size — saved right to their profile.

AI Champions Hub — built with Power Apps code apps, ready to deploy to your tenant.
